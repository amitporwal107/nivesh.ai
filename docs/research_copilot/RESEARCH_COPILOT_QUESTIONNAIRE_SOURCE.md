Absolutely. For **Nivesh AI Research Copilot**, the best approach is to design the chatbot around a comprehensive **question taxonomy**. This helps users discover what they can ask and helps you define intents, tools, data sources, and guardrails.

# Nivesh AI Research Copilot — Complete Query Questionnaire

## 1. Company Discovery & Overview

### Basic questions
- What does **[Company]** do?
- Give me a complete overview of **[Company]**.
- What are the company's major business segments?
- Where does the company generate its revenue?
- Who are its major customers?
- Who are its competitors?
- What is the company's competitive advantage?
- What are the major growth drivers?
- What are the biggest risks?
- Explain this company in simple language.
- Give me the investment/research case for this company.

### Business understanding
- How does **[Company]** make money?
- What is the company's business model?
- Which business segment is growing fastest?
- Which segment is most profitable?
- What percentage of revenue comes from each business?
- How dependent is the company on one product/customer/region?
- What are the key KPIs I should track?

---

# 2. Financial Analysis

## Revenue & Growth

- How has revenue grown over the last 5 years?
- Show quarterly revenue growth.
- Compare QoQ and YoY revenue growth.
- Is revenue growth accelerating or slowing?
- What caused the latest revenue growth?
- Which segment contributed most to growth?
- Is growth organic or acquisition-driven?

## Profitability

- How has EBITDA changed over time?
- How are operating margins trending?
- Why did margins improve?
- Why did margins decline?
- Compare EBITDA margins with peers.
- Is PAT growth sustainable?
- What is driving profit growth?

## Returns

- What is the company's ROE?
- What is the ROCE trend?
- Is ROCE improving?
- Compare ROE and ROCE with competitors.
- Why is ROE high/low?
- Is the company creating shareholder value?

## Cash Flow

- Is the company generating free cash flow?
- Compare PAT versus operating cash flow.
- Is profit converting into cash?
- Show free cash flow trends.
- Is there a cash-flow quality concern?
- Is working capital increasing?

---

# 3. Balance Sheet & Financial Health

### Debt
- How much debt does the company have?
- Is debt increasing?
- What is the debt-to-equity ratio?
- Can the company comfortably service its debt?
- What is the interest coverage ratio?
- When does the debt mature?

### Liquidity
- What is the current ratio?
- Does the company have sufficient liquidity?
- How much cash does it have?
- Is there a liquidity risk?

### Capital Allocation
- How does management allocate capital?
- Is the company investing efficiently?
- Has management made good acquisitions?
- How much capital expenditure is planned?
- Are buybacks or dividends expected?

---

# 4. Quarterly Results Intelligence ⭐

### Results summary
- Summarize the latest quarterly results.
- What were the key highlights?
- What were the key negatives?
- What surprised the market?
- Was this quarter better or worse than expected?

### Detailed analysis
- Compare this quarter with the previous quarter.
- Compare this quarter with the same quarter last year.
- What changed compared to the previous quarter?
- Which metrics improved?
- Which metrics deteriorated?
- What should I monitor next quarter?

### AI insight
- What are the 5 most important takeaways?
- Give me a bullish interpretation.
- Give me a bearish interpretation.
- What is the biggest concern in these results?
- What could be a catalyst after these results?

---

# 5. Earnings Call & Management Intelligence 🎙️

### Management commentary
- Summarize the latest earnings call.
- What is management optimistic about?
- What is management concerned about?
- What guidance did management provide?
- Did management change its guidance?

### Historical comparison
- Compare management commentary across the last 4 quarters.
- Has management's tone changed?
- What promises has management made?
- Which promises were delivered?
- Which promises were not delivered?

### Deep research
- What questions did analysts repeatedly ask?
- What questions did management avoid answering?
- What are the most important management statements?
- Find contradictions in management commentary.

---

# 6. Valuation Analysis

### Basic valuation
- Is **[Company]** expensive or cheap?
- What is its current P/E?
- What is its historical P/E range?
- What is its P/B ratio?
- What is its EV/EBITDA?

### Comparative valuation
- Compare valuation with peers.
- Why does this company trade at a premium?
- Why is this company trading at a discount?
- Compare current valuation with its historical average.

### Valuation scenarios
- What assumptions justify the current valuation?
- What growth is the market pricing in?
- What happens if earnings grow by 15%, 20%, or 25%?
- What are the major valuation risks?

---

# 7. Peer Comparison ⭐

- Compare **Company A vs Company B**.
- Which company has better growth?
- Which has better margins?
- Which has better ROE/ROCE?
- Which has lower debt?
- Which has better valuation?
- Which has stronger institutional ownership?
- Which has better earnings momentum?

### Full comparison

> Compare **HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank and Axis Bank** across fundamentals, valuation, growth, profitability, asset quality and institutional ownership.

---

# 8. Shareholding & Smart Money Intelligence 💰

## Promoters

- Has promoter holding increased?
- Has promoter holding decreased?
- Has promoter pledging increased?
- Show promoter holding over time.
- Are promoters buying or selling?

## FII

- Has FII ownership increased?
- Which stocks are FIIs accumulating?
- Which stocks are FIIs selling?
- Show FII ownership changes over 4 quarters.

## DII / Mutual Funds

- Which stocks are mutual funds accumulating?
- Which DIIs are increasing exposure?
- Which funds recently entered this company?
- Which institutions recently exited?

### Smart Money questions

- Is smart money accumulating this stock?
- Is institutional buying supporting the price?
- Find stocks with increasing FII and DII ownership.
- Find stocks where institutions are accumulating despite price weakness.

---

# 9. Corporate Events & News Intelligence ⚡

- What are the latest important events?
- What happened recently?
- Why is the stock moving today?
- What was the impact of this announcement?
- Is this news materially important?

### Event analysis

- Summarize this corporate announcement.
- What is the financial impact?
- What are the risks?
- Who benefits from this event?
- Compare this event with previous similar events.

### Historical context

- Has something similar happened before?
- What happened to the stock historically after similar events?
- Is this event positive, negative or neutral based on available evidence?

---

# 10. Technical Analysis

### Trend
- What is the current trend?
- Is the stock bullish or bearish?
- Is it above the 20, 50 and 200 DMA?
- Is the trend strengthening or weakening?

### Momentum
- What is the RSI?
- Is momentum improving?
- Is there positive divergence?
- Is there negative divergence?

### Volume
- Is volume supporting the move?
- Is delivery percentage increasing?
- Is there unusual volume?
- Is institutional participation visible?

### Price structure
- Is the stock near a 52-week high?
- Is the stock near a 52-week low?
- Is there a consolidation?
- Is there a breakout pattern?

# 22. Hypothesis Testing & Validation 🧪

A research analyst should be able to ask:

> Test this hypothesis historically.

Examples:

- Does increasing FII ownership lead to better performance?
- Do earnings surprises predict future returns?
- Do high delivery volumes indicate sustained momentum?
- Does ROCE improvement predict outperformance?

The output should include:

- Sample size
- Time period
- Win rate
- Average outcome
- Median outcome
- Maximum drawdown
- Statistical confidence
- Limitations
