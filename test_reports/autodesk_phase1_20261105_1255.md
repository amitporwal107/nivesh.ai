## Summary
A separate FastAPI app (`uvicorn autodesk.main:app`) reusing the backend's code: five deterministic agents (Tech
Support, AI Trader, Trading Assistant, Risk & Operations, Middle Office), an orchestrator with persisted tasks,
handoffs and an append-only event log, a deterministic risk engine, approvals bound to the exact plan version (plan
hash), a "Stop new entries" control, and a simulator that replays past sessions on point-in-time data. No orders, fills
or positions (Phase 2). Verified here: 99 unit/integration tests, and a real-data run of the scanner → strategy →
risk → approval chain, of a two-session replay and of the HTTP API.

## Findings while verifying (fixed here unless stated)
1. Shared `services/risk_gate/estimate._worst_point` breaks payoff ties on float noise: an iron condor of 65-lot legs
   showed a loss of ₹63,694 at 9 lots and ₹19,479 at 10 (costs charged at a price of 0). Worked around in
   `autodesk/control/sizing._options_loss`; **the shared function is NOT changed** (it feeds the live risk gate) —
   reported to the owner.
2. With flat volatility an iron condor with shorts at one expected move never meets the owner's credit rule (credit
   after costs ≥ width/3; ratio 0.21–0.30). The Trading Assistant now searches inward for the widest shorts that meet it.
3. The app ran without CSRF / security-header middleware when that import failed; it now refuses to start.
4. Sector lookup used a wrong path and emptied both sources; fixed.
