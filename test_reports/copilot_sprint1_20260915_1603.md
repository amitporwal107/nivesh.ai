## Summary
Sprint 1 of the V5 design review (artifact "Nivesh V5 Design Review"):

- **C1** The copilot reveal is paced to token arrival (MIN_CPS 140, catch-up ≤ 450 ms, post-stream hold capped at 1.2 s) instead of a fixed 10 s window; click on the bubble or Esc shows everything at once; widget sections build in ~1.2 s instead of ~11 s.
- **C2** The chat page and the dock now render the `route` frame as an agent ribbon, `thinking` frames as a live step list, and `done.follow_ups` as "Ask next" chips that send on tap. New shared component `components/chat/StreamStatus.tsx`.
- **C5** One exported `WIDGET_TYPES` (19 entries) in `ChatWidget.tsx`, imported by page and dock (the dock's own 11-entry copy rendered a blank turn for the other eight).
- **C8** Stop button (AbortSignal through `streamSend`) keeps the partial answer locally with a "Stopped" tag and "Try again"; mid-stream errors are kept the same way instead of vanishing.
- **C11** User-facing "NIDP" copy replaced: review card pill "LIVE DATA", analysis line "READ n HOLDINGS · LIVE", sidebar "₹ x L · synced".
- **A1** `ErrorState` leads with a plain-language message per `ApiError` kind, shows `Reference <correlationId>` when present, and puts the raw message (e.g. the Zod dump) behind a "Details" disclosure.
- **A2** Homepage phone header: brand, Sign in and CTA no longer wrap (Sign in yields below 400 px). Login stacks to one column on phones, the eyebrow wraps, and the desktop product tour plus its floating "Ask the copilot" demo pill are hidden pre-sign-in on phones (the wrapper's inline `display` moved to CSS so the phone rule can apply).
