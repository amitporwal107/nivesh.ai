**Masking.** The staging run used the owner's REAL live broker book. Under the standing rule "no real positions in reports
pushed to GitHub", the position lines below are replaced by placeholders: symbols become A–G, and quantities, averages and
rupee amounts are left out. The broker account id is shortened. Status codes, counts, matches and the restore lines are
copied as printed. The full unmasked output stays in the session scratchpad and is not committed.
