## Summary
A Chartink-inspired scan editor at Market Desk › Scan Builder (Columns · Filters · Group/Sort, Design · Result, Run · Save ·
Copy · Cancel) whose engine runs on our own data only: NSE end-of-day bars (split/bonus adjusted) from the NIDP warehouse,
today's candle from Kite (live → Kite close → EOD, the owner's 29 Oct rule), fundamentals from the NIDP feature store. Daily,
weekly and monthly timeframes (intraday is the next step, owner 3 Nov). The Result re-runs every 10 s while the market is open.
Behind the `scan_builder` feature flag (allowlist: owner).
