**App:** `GET /api/move-odds/diagnostics` (session cookie + `require_feature("move_odds")`) →
`{data: {source_sha256, study{first_signal_day, last_signal_day, universe, cost_round_trip, rule, baseline, tested, validated},
setups[{id, name, status: not_validated|research_only_insufficient_sample, status_label, headline, explanation,

503 `diagnostics_unavailable`, no numbers. Not allowlisted → 403 `feature_not_enabled`; no session → 401.
**V5:** a "Research diagnostics" section at the foot of the Move odds screen; loading, error-with-retry, and loaded states.
