## Summary
PRD "Ten-Percent Days v6" Final Product Principle: the platform must answer four separate questions, and a movement
probability must not read as a buy signal. The page today ranks one direction per tab with the opposite as a faded
secondary column, which is exactly the reading the evidence says is wrong — in the 2025 test the top decile by +5%
score touched +5% on 19% of days and ALSO touched −5% on 14.7%, and buying at the open and selling at the close
averaged −0.23%.

Owner decision (AskUserQuestion, same day): **two size tabs, both directions shown as equals, ranked by the larger of
the two.**

## Contract
**No API change.** The screen already loads all four heads; each row carries `p` and `p_opposite`.
- Tabs become **5% move** and **10% move**. For the 5% tab a row's up figure is p_up5_1d and its down figure is
  p_down5_1d; same shape for 10%.
- Rows rank by **the larger of the two published estimates**. This is a description of two numbers, not a new combined
  probability — no model output is invented, and the column is labelled as such.
- A **direction reading** is derived from the same two numbers by a stated rule: `two-way` when the smaller is at
  least 60% of the larger; otherwise `up-leaning` / `down-leaning`. It is a comparison of two estimates, never a
  direction forecast — the direction model does not exist yet (PRD Phase 3).
- A **"What these numbers answer"** panel states the four questions with their honest current status: movement yes
  (with the calibration record), direction not predicted, expected net return not modelled, out-of-sample edge not
  demonstrated.
- History keeps a direction toggle inside its own view, since its ranking is per head.
